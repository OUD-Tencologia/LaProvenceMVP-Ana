# LGPD Compliance Notes

## Personal Data Processed

The interface may collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, CPF, wedding/list metadata, optional images, and purchase-related information.

## Purpose

Data is collected to create wedding lists, allow guests to send gifts, identify purchases, support payment processing, and provide customer support.

## Minimization

Only request personal data when needed for account creation, list management, checkout, fraud prevention, or payment provider requirements.

## Data Subject Requests

Operational requests for access, correction, export, and deletion must be handled together with the backend, because the browser is not the system of record.

## Third Parties

PagBank and Google reCAPTCHA may receive checkout-related technical and identity information necessary for payment processing and bot prevention.
